About

I am an urban and economic geographer. I am building a national database of the urban renewal projects that used Section 112 credits, and writing a white paper and policy brief on the program for the Smart Cities Research Lab at Trinity College.

I am also writing about hospitals, housing and heat between Harvard and Mission Hill, and about endowments, pensions and fiduciary duty in the US.

I earned a PhD in Geography from the University of Arizona in 2023 and was a Postdoctoral Fellow in City and Regional Planning at Cornell from 2023 to 2026.

Section 112 database

Section 112 of the Housing Act of 1949, added in 1959, let land purchases by colleges, universities and hospitals count toward a city's local share of an urban renewal project. This database records the 123 projects that used it across 23 editions of the federal directory, June 1955 to June 1966. Each value shows which edition it belongs to and how it was established.
