Section 112 Project Atlas

Urban renewal projects that used Section 112 college, university and hospital credits, 1955–1966.

About this atlas

Each marker is a federal urban renewal project that counted land purchases by a college, university or hospital toward the city's local share of project costs under Section 112 of the Housing Act of 1949 (added in 1959). It shows each project's Section 112 credit, status and basis in every edition of Urban Renewal Project Characteristics from June 1955 to June 1966.

Sources

Credits, status and basis: U.S. Housing and Home Finance Agency and U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, Urban Renewal Project Characteristics, 1955–1966 (semi-annual). Values marked inferred were filled from adjacent editions. No June 1963 edition was found, and the June 1966 edition omits projects still in planning.

Displacement figures and urban renewal shapefiles: Renewing Inequality, Digital Scholarship Lab, University of Richmond (CC BY-NC). Projects without a boundary there are shown at the city center.
